Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2025  |  Effective: January 1, 2025

At BUILDERS' POINT DEPO LTD (BPD LTD), we are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you register for a trade account, place an order, or contact our team:

  • Full name, company name, and job title
  • Business and delivery addresses, phone numbers
  • Email address and account login credentials
  • Payment and billing information (processed by our secure payment partners)
  • VAT/Tax registration numbers for trade accounts
  • Order history and procurement preferences

We also collect technical data automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited — to improve security and platform performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing and fulfilling orders, deliveries, and invoices
  • Managing your trade account and credit arrangements
  • Sending order confirmations, updates, and support responses
  • Sending relevant promotions and bulk pricing alerts (with your consent)
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring platform security
  • Meeting our legal and financial record-keeping obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with:

  • Logistics Partners — for site and depot deliveries
  • Payment Processors — secure, PCI-compliant gateways only
  • Legal Authorities — where required by law or court order
  • Professional Advisors — accountants and legal counsel under confidentiality

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and for a minimum of 7 years after your last transaction, as required by financial regulations. You may request earlier deletion where legally permitted — we will respond within 30 days.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to keep your session, cart, and preferences working. We also use analytics cookies (anonymised) to understand how the platform is used. You may manage non-essential cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to marketing or data profiling
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format

To exercise any right, email us at privacy@bpdltd.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We protect your data with SSL/TLS encryption, strict access controls, regular security audits, and staff training. While we take every reasonable precaution, no digital system is completely risk-free — we encourage the use of strong, unique passwords on your account.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated to registered account holders via email. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any updated policy.
